## Fonts
- The site sets **Sukhumvit Set** (`--wp--preset--font-family--sukhumvit-set`). Sukhumvit Set is an Apple system font.
- Suggested web-safe stand-ins: Sarabun or IBM Plex Sans Thai.

## Tone
- Friendly and playful Thai, with self-deprecating "มนุษย์เป็ด / เป็ดพรีเมี่ยม" humor (generalists who upskill).
- Practical, hands-on, aimed at business users rather than developers. Casual phrasing such as "555+" and emoji.
